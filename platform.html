HOW IT WORKS

Automate document processing in six steps.

Collect documents, extract and format data, validate results, review exceptions and deliver records to your systems.

01 / 06

Acquire

Collect documents from your sources.

Upload PDFs and images, import Outlook emails and attachments, or submit documents through the API.

Choose how documents arrive

  • Upload files directly
  • Import Outlook email or attachments
  • Submit through the customer API

02 / 06

Extract

Extract document data with AI.

Capture fields, tables and repeated sections from your documents. Define what to extract without training a model for each layout.

Configure the record

  • Name the fields and their data types
  • Describe the values to capture
  • Include repeated rows and sections

03 / 06

Transform

Format data for your systems.

Map extracted values to your output fields. Standardise dates and numbers, apply lookups and calculate values.

Shape the output

  • Map source fields to your record
  • Standardise dates and number formats
  • Apply lookups and calculations

04 / 06

Validate

Validate data with business rules.

Check required fields, ranges, allowed values and dates. Set warnings or blocking rules for records that need attention.

Put your rules in place

  • Check required fields and allowed values
  • Set ranges and date rules
  • Choose warning or blocking behaviour

Blocking failures need attention before delivery.

05 / 06

Review

Review and correct exceptions.

Check extracted values against the source document and correct them before delivery. Review all documents, focus on exceptions or omit routine review.

Choose your review policy

  • Review all documents or exceptions
  • Check and correct values against the source
  • Check the original document alongside extracted data

Blocking failures still need attention. Business approvals stay in your downstream systems.

06 / 06

Deliver

Deliver data to connected systems.

Send eligible records to your applications, databases or cloud storage. Track delivery status and use supported retries when needed.

Connect the next step

  • Choose an application or storage destination
  • Configure the record to deliver
  • Check delivery status and supported retries

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Platform FAQs.

Have a specific process in mind?
Let’s talk about your workflow ↗

Do I need to train a model for every document layout?

No. Zero-shot extraction uses your configured fields and guidance without a separate model for each layout. Test your workflow with representative documents before activation.

Can I check the workflow before activating it?

Yes. Test extraction, transformations and validation with representative documents. Adjust your configuration before activating the workflow.

Does every document need human review?

No. Choose review for all documents or exceptions, or omit routine review. Blocking validation failures still need attention before delivery.

Is document review the same as business approval?

No. Document review checks and corrects extracted data. Business decisions and approvals take place in your downstream systems.

Can DocAcquire connect to our existing systems?

Yes. Use configured destinations, connectors and API access to send records to your applications and storage.

Which deployment options are available?

Choose Managed Cloud, Private Cloud or On-Premises deployment to fit your infrastructure and data requirements.
