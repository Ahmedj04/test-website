A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you
get started.

Have a specific process in mind?
Let’s talk about it ↗

What is a workflow in DocAcquire?

A workflow is the process each document follows in DocAcquire, from receipt through delivery into your next business system. It connects ingestion, extraction, transformation, validation, review and delivery.

What do I set up first?

Start by choosing what to capture, including individual fields and repeated information such as line items. Then check the mappings, validation and results with a representative document before activating the workflow.

How are transformation and validation different?

Transformation converts extracted values into the format you need. Validation applies required checks and business rules to that data. Both happen before the review and delivery stages.

Is data review the same as business approval?

Yes. Choose review for all documents or exceptions, or omit routine review. People can check and correct document values; blocking validation failures still need attention. This is data review. Invoice authorisation, payment approval and other business decisions take place in your downstream process.

Where do the results go?

Deliver eligible records to a configured destination such as Zoho Books, a webhook, SQL Server or cloud storage. Delivery completes the DocAcquire document workflow; the receiving system handles the next business activity.

Can I test before processing live documents?

Yes. Review and test your mappings, validation and results against a representative document before activating your workflow.
